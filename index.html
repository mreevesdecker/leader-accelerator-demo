Where this takes you

Lead yourself. Lead others. Twelve questions that build both.

Every module asks one leadership question twice — first of you (Lead Self), then of your team (Lead Others). You master each tool in your own seat before you’re asked to lead anyone else through it. Twelve questions later, you haven’t taken a course — you’ve built how your company leads.

Two tracks, one mirror. Module for module, Lead Others asks the question Lead Self just answered — Who am I? becomes Who are we?, What do I own? becomes How do I hand off ownership? You never teach a tool you aren’t already living.

Five threads, woven through both tracks

Every module pulls on one of five threads — the same five whether the question is asked of you or of your team.

Section 1

What I Own

Role clarity · C.A.R. · Commander’s Intent · understanding ownership.
Outcome: you can say what your seat produces and which calls are yours — and so can everyone around you.

Section 2

How I Decide

Decision tree · decision rights · ownership as a two-way street.
Outcome: decisions get made at your level, inside a boundary everyone trusts — no more waiting on a yes.

Section 3

How We Run Together

Weekly Sync · cadence & pacing · objectives · bets · roadmap alignment.
Outcome: a rhythm nobody has to chase, aligned to a roadmap the whole company can see.

Section 4

Who We’re Becoming

Personal roadmap · identity · team cohesion · culture.
Outcome: a future worth staying for — for you, and for the team you’re building.

Section 5

How We Think

Abundance · Systems · Strategy · Adaptability mindsets · first principles · pre/post-mortems.
Outcome: thinking that sharpens with every rep — the mindsets that outlast any single tool.
